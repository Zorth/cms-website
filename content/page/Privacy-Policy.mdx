## 2. What Personal Data We Collect and Why

We only collect data necessary to organize community gaming events, manage our non-profit association, and process memberships:

### A. Event Registrations (e.g. D&D, Boardgame Nights)
- **Data collected**: Name/nickname, email address, chosen game/group table.
- **Purpose**: Managing table player counts, sending confirmation emails, and allowing you to cancel your spot.
- **Legal basis**: Performance of a contract / legitimate interest (Art. 6.1(b) and (f) GDPR).
- **Retention**: Event registration logs are retained for the season and periodically cleared.

### B. User Accounts & Dragon Authentication (Clerk)
- **Data collected**: Name, email address, profile picture (avatar), authentication tokens, user role (`user`, `member`, `dragon`).
- **Purpose**: Authenticating into the website, accessing member benefits, and managing community council permissions.
- **Legal basis**: Performance of a contract (Art. 6.1(b) GDPR).
- **Service provider**: **Clerk Inc.** (Data processed under EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

### C. Membership Subscriptions & Payments (Stripe)
- **Data collected**: Name, email, billing address, VAT/tax ID (for businesses), subscription status, payment history. We do **not** store complete credit card or payment credentials on our servers.
- **Purpose**: Administering your annual Kobold membership (€10/year) and complying with Belgian accounting, tax, and VAT regulations.
- **Legal basis**: Performance of a contract and legal obligation under Belgian tax law (Art. 6.1(b) and (c) GDPR).
- **Service provider**: **Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd.**

### D. Community Communications (Resend / Discord)
- **Data collected**: Email address for automated transaction/confirmation receipts via Resend.
- **Purpose**: Sending necessary service notifications (event ticket confirmation, cancellation tokens). We do not send unsolicited marketing spam.

## 3. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website is built to be privacy-first and does not use third-party behavioral advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

- **Essential / Strictly Necessary Cookies**:
  - **Authentication (Clerk)**: Session tokens (`__session`, `__client_uat`) necessary for keeping you logged in securely.
  - **Security & Cloud Infrastructure**: Load balancing and fraud prevention tokens.
  - **Cookie Consent**: A local storage item (`tarragon_cookie_consent`) recording your acceptance of the cookie notice.
- **Analytics**: If minimal anonymous aggregated traffic analytics are enabled, they run in privacy-preserving mode without fingerprinting personal information.

## 6. Your Rights Under GDPR

Under European data protection law, you have the following rights:

1. **Right of Access (Art. 15 GDPR)**: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
2. **Right to Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)**: You can update incorrect or incomplete information via your user profile or by contacting us.
3. **Right to Erasure / "Right to be Forgotten" (Art. 17 GDPR)**: You can request the deletion of your account and personal data, except where retention is required by Belgian law (e.g., financial/tax accounting).
4. **Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18 GDPR)**: You may request that we limit the processing of your data in certain circumstances.
5. **Right to Data Portability (Art. 20 GDPR)**: You can receive your data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
6. **Right to Object (Art. 21 GDPR)**: You can object at any time to processing based on legitimate interests.

To exercise any of these rights, please send an email to **[privacy@tarragon.be](mailto:privacy@tarragon.be)** or **[contact@tarragon.be](mailto:contact@tarragon.be)**. We will respond within 30 days without charge.
